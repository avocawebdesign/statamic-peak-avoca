The facts this site's pages may state. Read Avoca's `vendor/avocadesign/statamic-tools/recipe/facts.md` before you write or change copy. This file is for the team: it is never served, and nothing on the site links to it.

Each fact is one line, then in brackets where it came from, when it was checked, and `public`, `approved` or `internal`, such as `- Founded in 2006 (source: discovery call; checked 2026-03-03; public)`.

## The business

List when it started, where it is and where it works, who runs it, and its accreditations and memberships.

## What it offers

List the services and products, and any prices the site states.

## Numbers

List the numbers a page may quote, such as years trading, jobs done or people employed, each with the date it was counted.

## Customer outcomes

List what changed for a customer, each pointing at the published case study or testimonial entry, or marked approved with the date the client approved it.

## Know-how

List what the client knows from doing the work, in their words.

## Not yet

List the sectors, places and services the client wants but hasn't worked in yet. Pages offer these, and never claim experience of them.

## Open questions

List each `[NEEDS PROOF]` still open, with the page it is on.
